## 👋 About me

I’m Ayush, a software engineer focused on **backend systems, financial infrastructure, and AI tooling**. I enjoy taking a product from an early idea through to software that people use and depend on.

Currently, I lead engineering at **PivotMoney**, building an investing platform for Indian and overseas investors. My work spans financial integrations, transaction workflows, reconciliation, and production infrastructure. Previously at **Turing**, I built APIs and evaluation tools for large language model workflows.

I work primarily with Python, Django, PostgreSQL, and React. I’m particularly interested in the details that make systems dependable: how data stays consistent, how failures are handled, and how a system can be understood when something goes wrong.

🎓 I graduated from **IIT Kharagpur** in 2024 with a dual B.Tech and M.Tech degree.
